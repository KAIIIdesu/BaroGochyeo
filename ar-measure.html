AR measure

Measure a pothole or crack on the ground by tapping its two edges.

How to measure
  1. Stand on the sidewalk, about 1 m from the hazard.
  2. Move the phone slowly until a white ring appears on the ground.
  3. Put the cross on one edge and tap. Then tap the opposite edge.
